I'm Ke Haoran (柯浩然), a product manager who also writes the code.

I take a product from the first sketch to production on my own: the research and the decisions, the interface design, the front end, the back end, and the servers it runs on. Doing every step myself keeps the distance between an idea and a working release short.

Most of my work now is with AI agents: deciding what they should do, and building the tools that make what they produce good enough to ship.
